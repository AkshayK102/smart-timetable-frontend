Create a clash-free timetable for all batches with one click.

How it works

  • A teacher is never placed in two classes at the same time.
  • A batch never has two subjects at the same time.
  • Lectures of a subject are spread across different days where possible.
  • Generating again replaces the existing timetable of all batches.

Before you generate

Each batch has 36 periods per week (6 days, 6 periods each).
