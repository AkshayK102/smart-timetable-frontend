About Smart Timetable Scheduler

Smart Timetable Scheduler is a web-based system designed to simplify and automate the process of creating and managing college timetables.

The Problem

Creating college timetables manually can be time-consuming and may result in teacher, classroom and schedule conflicts.

Our Solution

This system provides a centralized platform where administrators can manage academic information and generate timetables while considering important scheduling constraints.

Key Features

Important features of the scheduling system.

📅

Smart Timetable

Generate timetables while considering subjects, teachers, rooms and time slots.

👨‍🏫

Teacher Management

Administrators can manage teachers and their assigned subjects.

🏫

Room Management

Manage classrooms, laboratories and their available capacity.

📚

Subject Management

Manage subjects and their required weekly teaching hours.

🎓

Batch Management

Organize students according to course, semester and division.

⚡

Conflict Checking

The scheduling process considers teacher, room and timetable conflicts.

Two Types of Users

Admin

Admin manages the complete timetable system.

  • ✓ Manage teachers
  • ✓ Manage subjects
  • ✓ Manage batches
  • ✓ Manage rooms
  • ✓ Generate timetable
  • ✓ View generated timetable

User

Users can view their timetable and account information.

  • ✓ View personal timetable
  • ✓ View today's classes
  • ✓ View weekly schedule
  • ✓ View profile
